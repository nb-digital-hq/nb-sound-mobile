# Code of Conduct

NB Sound Mobile is a community project. People take part from very different contexts and levels of experience,
including people contributing to an open-source project for the first time. The project commits to making that
participation possible without hostility.

## Our standards

* Respectful and professional treatment in issues, pull requests, reviews and every project channel.
* Criticism is aimed at the work, never at the person.
* Patience with people who are learning.
* Harassment, threats and derogatory comments about personal characteristics are not tolerated.

These standards apply equally to occasional contributors and to maintainers.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space and when an individual officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Reports are sent privately by email to **nbdigitalsas@gmail.com** with a subject that starts with
`[NB Sound Mobile][Conducta]`. Messages without that prefix are very likely not to be read. Every report is
acknowledged, investigated discreetly, and the identity of the reporter is kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
Decisions are proportionate and explained to the person concerned.
